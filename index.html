    What's the Shiny Charm?

    The Shiny Charm is a key item awarded for completing a game's Pokédex. Just having it in your bag roughly triples your shiny odds in most games, which is why every method above lists its odds both with and without it. It's a grind to earn, but it pays for itself on every hunt afterward.

    Always double-check availability before a long hunt — version exclusives aren't factored in, and a few pools (SOS especially) are messy by nature.
